# Role Definitions & Privilege Matrix
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Classification:** RESTRICTED / DEFENSE SPECIFICATION  

---

## 1. Role Definitions

### 1. Personnel (Soldier / Constable / Officer - Individual User)
- **Scope**: Sovereign access to own data only.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Submit voluntary self-assessments (PHQ-4 screening, sleep rating, operational stress level, personal reflections).
  - View personal longitudinal wellness and stress trends.
  - Access self-care recommendations, breathing exercises, and confidential mental wellness resources.
- **Restrictions**:
  - Strictly zero access to peer data, cohort statistics, or administrative tools.
  - Cannot be queried by commander directly.

### 2. Welfare Officer (Psychologist / Unit Medical Officer / Welfare Staff)
- **Scope**: Assigned battalion or company cohort only.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Access prioritized alert queue generated by the Game-Theoretic Alert Engine.
  - Inspect pseudonymized behavioral metrics (`pseudo_id`, e.g. `PX-7821`) and explainability breakdowns.
  - Recommend clinical interventions, peer buddy assignment, or leave prioritization.
  - Initiate audited "Break-Glass" re-identification under life-safety emergency criteria.
- **Restrictions**:
  - Cannot access personnel records outside assigned unit.
  - Real names and service numbers remain hidden/masked by default.
  - All queries are actively monitored by the Access-Pattern IDS.

### 3. Commander (Company Commander / Battalion Commandant)
- **Scope**: Entire battalion or tactical unit, **at aggregate level only**.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Strategic overview of unit operational readiness, aggregated stress heatmaps, leave utilization curves, and deployment hardship indexes.
  - Evidence-based workload balancing and rest-and-recuperation (R&R) planning.
- **Restrictions**:
  - **Zero access to individual records or pseudonymized records.**
  - Cohort queries returning fewer than $k=5$ individuals are automatically suppressed by the $k$-Anonymity guard.
  - Cannot identify which specific soldier is experiencing burnout or high stress.

### 4. Security & System Administrator
- **Scope**: System health, infrastructure maintenance, audit chain verification, and IDS alert management.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Monitor API gateway latency, database connection pools, and service status.
  - Review access-pattern IDS anomaly alerts (e.g., rogue officer queries).
  - Perform cryptographic integrity verification of the SHA-256 tamper-evident hash chain.
- **Restrictions**:
  - **Zero access to wellness, psychological, or clinical telemetry by default.**
  - The database stores sensitive fields encrypted with keys held in application memory, preventing DB admins from viewing raw personnel data.
  - This structural separation of infrastructure from data visibility serves as the primary trust pillar for the armed forces.
